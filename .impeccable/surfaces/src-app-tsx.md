# Surface brief: StartApp homepage

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: business owner judging whether StartApp is a serious software house. Action: talk to the team (WhatsApp).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A long editorial homepage where oversized type and real photographs carry credibility, refusing the landing-page arrangement of centered hero plus repeated card grids. Direction is brief-pinned (globant.com/es language), so no concept roll was run.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black (#0A0A0A) and warm off-white (#F5F5F2) fields alternating, one committed orange field (#F2542D, from the logo mark) for the marquee and closing call. Geist display at weight 500 with tight tracking, Geist Mono only for data labels. Square corners, hairline rules, no shadows, no glass, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor understands StartApp builds and keeps running real systems, believes it because four products are in production and 200+ clients use them, and acts by writing on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Transparent header with white logo left, five links and a "Hablemos" button right. Three-line headline at ~8vw across the left 80%. Subtext and two actions under it. A full-width photo strip along the bottom edge, darkened, with a slow constellation of the four real products linked to a central StartApp node.

FORM: Editorial long-scroll: hero, marquee, capability list with switching diagram panel, ledger-style metrics, four alternating case studies, dark manifesto, scroll-drawn timeline, typographic proof, orange call band, contact, oversized footer. Brief-pinned; no seed key (no roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
