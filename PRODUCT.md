## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized companies in Argentina and Latin America (distributors, nautical clubs, tourism operators, service businesses) who need to digitize operations with custom software, and who judge a software house by how serious and established it looks. [inferred]

## Product Purpose

StartApp is a software house in Posadas, Misiones, Argentina. It designs and builds management systems, custom applications and mobile apps, and supports them after launch. The site must make StartApp read as an international-grade technology company and drive visitors to talk to the team (WhatsApp is the real contact channel).

## Positioning

A small software house that keeps running the systems it ships: real products in production (water distribution management, self-service ordering app, nautical club management, tourism app for Puerto Iguazú), maintained and evolved continuously, with permanent support.

## Operating Context

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only facts present in the existing site may be stated as facts: 320+ clients, 3000+ daily users, Argentina and Latin America reach, the four projects below, address, hours, phone, Facebook and Instagram links.
- Projects (real, keep links): Reparto de Agua (repartodeagua.com), Autogestion Agua (Play Store), Guarderia Nautica / Capri (Play Store), Turismo Iguazu (Play Store). Encuest.ar was removed by the user and must not appear as a project card.

## Product Principles

1. Say only what is true; scale and craft carry the credibility, not invented claims.
2. Real projects in production are the proof; present them as case studies, not thumbnails.
3. One accent, used with intent. Typography and whitespace do the work.
4. Every section has its own composition; no repeated card grids.
5. Motion follows scroll, stays slow, and respects reduced-motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled form fields, reduced-motion support, 44px touch targets. Spanish (es-AR) voice, voseo.
